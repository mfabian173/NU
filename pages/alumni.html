Alumni

Celebrating the climbers who built our legacy and continue to inspire us today.

Our Climbing Family

Since our founding in 2014, over 100 climbers have been part of the Northeastern Climbing Team. Our alumni have gone on to pursue careers in engineering, medicine, finance, outdoor recreation, and beyond—but they all share the bond of being part of this incredible community. Many continue to climb, compete, and give back to the sport they love.

Notable Alumni

Former team members who helped shape our program and continue to make an impact.

MB

Mike Beltran

Founding Member • Class of 2017

One of the original founders who helped establish the team in 2014. Led the team to its first 2nd place finish at Nationals.

JH

Jessica Huang

Former Captain • Class of 2020

Two-time regional champion who helped grow the team's presence in the USA Climbing Collegiate Series.

RK

Ryan Kowalski

Former President • Class of 2021

Expanded team partnerships and established the sponsorship program that continues to support athletes today.

SL

Sarah Lee

Former VP • Class of 2019

Pioneered the team's outreach programs and helped establish connections with the recreational climbing club.

DM

Daniel Martinez

Former Captain • Class of 2022

Led training programs that elevated the team's technical skills and competitive performance at Divisionals.

AC

Amanda Chen

Former Captain • Class of 2023

Strengthened team culture and competitive drive, helping secure multiple podium finishes at regional competitions.
